## Open items
- **Waiting on the mentor (ADR 0018 §17):** default paper size (`letter` until answered); whether
  reviewers and client admins may see conversation and custodian names.
- **Required before production (BACKLOG):** report image pushed to our registry with
  `deploy/render-images.json` and report-worker deployment files; the sRGB2014 ICC profile verified
  against color.org; the rest of the pre-production list.
- **Not built (ADR 0018 §31):** export size, findings and tier detection in the report's access
  section; a per-scope unit count; `measure_report.py` at 1M units (cloud VM); report reproductions
  on old images; a standalone verifier distribution; collecting hostile-id conversations under their
  stand-in key.
- **Not recomputable offline (by design, docs/VERIFYING.md §5):** observations, renders, the
  snapshot, divergences; HTML and PDF are checked by digest (the HTML is reproducible from
  `report.json` per renderer version, the PDF only in the pinned image).
- Undiagnosed, seen once: a 265 ms loop block in the API middleware (HANDOFF, "Gotchas").
